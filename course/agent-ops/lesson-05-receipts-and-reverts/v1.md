# Receipts, versions and the right to be wrong

**Learning objective.** Leave a chain of evidence, and write your own undo condition before you
are trusted with autonomy.

## Receipts are the memory
Every decision writes one append-only row: what was seen, the quotes, the judge's answers, the
action and reason codes, the artifact hashes, the cost. Hash-chain the rows and verify at the end of
every run. A decision without a receipt is a bug of the same severity as a wrong action.

## The falsification clause
No publish without a revert gate — the metric, threshold, cohort size and window that will make the
system restore the previous version. No revert without a satisfied gate, either: undo is a claim and
it needs evidence too.

## Practice
For your last change, write the gate you would have needed, and the moment you would have known.
