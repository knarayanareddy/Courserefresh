# Apify as the input layer

**Learning objective.** Turn real pages into hashed snapshots with pinned actors, and describe
your cadence honestly.

## Snapshots before claims
Fetch first, validate the actor input against the actor schema, hash immediately, cache the bytes. Actor inputs are validated against the actor's input schema before a run starts. Every later claim points at a byte range in that
cached file, so "the source said this" is checkable months later — or five minutes later when two
sources disagree.

## Cadence honesty
An actor is not a websocket. Runs take 30–120 s; a schedule has jitter. Say the configured interval
and the measured jitter; never say real-time. The honest number is *source captured → decision
made*, whatever it turns out to be.

## Practice
- Where do you hash: before or after parsing? (before)
- What do you publish when two sources fight? (an escalation, not an average)
